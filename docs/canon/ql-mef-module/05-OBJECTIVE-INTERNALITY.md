# Part VII — Objective internality as an application of MEF

## 38. Objective internality is not a kernel primitive

The emerging notion of objective internality is a powerful **application domain** for QL/MEF, especially Square B and the articulation/becoming squares.

It should not become a required QL kernel object.

AIKit may own operational states such as:

```text
what exists
known-to-exist
askable
retrieved
focused
```

and provisional absence semantics such as:

```text
open
latent
unknown
irrelevant
bound
missing
```

The QL/MEF module may refract these states, reveal their relations, or help design their language. It does not need to canonise them as MEF sublenses.

---

## 39. Disclosure and concealment

Square A and L4 make it possible to analyse disclosure as a real design dimension:

- words can disclose or constrict;
- silence can preserve latitude or hide a missing dependency;
- named-but-unopened Skills can be more usable than invisible Skills or fully preloaded procedure;
- an information horizon can remain available without being loaded;
- a Project/world can be encountered as nested revealing/concealing regions.

These principles may guide AIKit and agentic seed design while remaining outside deterministic kernel semantics unless a precise operator is later developed.

---

## 40. Growth relation

Square C gives the module a natural way to read development:

```text
seed state S₀
    ↓
possible/intended becoming — L3
    ↓
actual unfolding — L3′
    ↓
empirical encounter/correction — L4′
    ↓
articulated/retained result — L5/L5′
    ↓
changed ground S₁
```

This is a refraction pattern, not a single mandatory agent loop.

It can be applied to Skills, Profiles, Agents/Agencies, Projects, Runs, or AIKit itself.
